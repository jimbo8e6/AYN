## The Game

Most RPGs of this era make you the chosen one. *The 7th Saga* makes you one of
seven candidates, and then lets the other six walk around the same world you
do, hunting the same runes, levelling up while you are levelling up. Meet one
and you fight them. They do not scale down to be fair about it.

That structure is the whole game and it is genuinely clever. Your choice of
character is not a stat spread, it is a decision about which six rivals you
have made. The apprentice you pick up as a companion may turn on you. The
world is small but it is *contested*, in a way almost nothing else on the
system attempted.

The problem is the balance. Random encounters hit hard, the rivals hit harder,
and the North American release adjusted enemy statistics upward from the
Japanese version, which is the sort of decision that turns a demanding game
into an unreasonable one. Progress is measured in grinding rather than in
discovery.

## The People

Developed by **Produce!** and published by **Enix** — the second Enix game in
this project already, and we have not left the number sign yet. It came out in
Japan as *Elnard* before the North American localisation retitled it.
