## The Game

![ACME Animation Factory logo](/games/acme-animation-factory/logo.png)

Not much I can say about this really, but I had to include it. It's not really a game, it's *Mario Paint* with a Looney Tunes license. It's mostly there for young creative kids to create their own Looney Tunes animations and colour in pictures. There's the odd mini game, similar to *Mario Paint*, but nothing to really write home about.

It was developed by **Probe Software** who were notable for a lot of arcade conversions for other home computer systems in the 80s, they dealt with console conversions of *Outrun* and *Mortal Kombat II* to several home consoles. They were eventually bought out by Acclaim in 1995 before going bankrupt in 2004. The name was resurrected in 2025 with attempts to buy Acclaim IPs, but nothing has come of it yet.

Published by **Sun Corporation of America**, the American branch of Japan's Sun Corporation, also known as Sunsoft. They developed and published loads of popular games like *Aero the Acrobat 1 & 2* for the SNES, *Blaster Master* for the NES, *Batman: The Video Game*, the SNES release of *Lemmings*, and some home console releases of *Myst*.

### Gameplay

Like I said, there isn't much to be said, there are some pre-set animations that can be set on different backgrounds to create Looney Tunes scenes, some blank pictures that can be coloured in, your own music creator/editor, and a couple of mini games, a Solitaire, and a Mix and Match card game where you have to find pairs of cards. This really was designed for artistic children who dreamed of becoming an animator.

### Graphics

Admittedly, the graphics are very nice, but you'd expect that from software focused primarily on graphics! The sprites are big, clear and well drawn, the animation is nice, and the UI for the painting and animation is clear and well presented, although the choice of colour wasn't my favourite, an orangey wood-effect background was a bit garish, but that was the style of the time I suppose!

### Sound and Music

No complaints here, it's your typical Looney Tunes jaunty music, the Looney Tunes opening theme is there, and everything else along with it, and whatever monstrosities you can create in the music creator.

## The People

There are some interesting things to find about the people behind the game at least, one of the game's designers, **Greg Michael**, leaned more into programming after this and worked on *Mortal Kombat II*, *Alien Trilogy* (my favourite shooter on the PS1), *Forsaken*, and *Crackdown 3* to name a few, but he worked double duty on this game and was Producer as well as design.

The game's second producer was a real Jack-of-all-trades working production, design, graphics, QA, and marketing across 46 games, but dropped off the radar in 2006.

The music was worked on by **Steve Collett** who is still very active to this day working on games like *Pagemaster*, *X-Men Mutant Academy*, *Street Fighter Alpha 3*, and most recently, *Powerwash Simulator 2*.
